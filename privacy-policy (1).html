1. About this application

Kids Learning Video Automation ("the App") is intended to help its authorized operator create and publish original educational and entertainment videos to connected social media accounts, including TikTok. This policy explains how information may be processed when the App is used.

2. Information we process

When you authorize a TikTok account, the App and its integration providers may process information necessary to connect and publish, such as your TikTok account identifier, basic profile information allowed by the permissions you grant, authorization tokens, publishing settings, uploaded media, captions, and publication status. We do not request your TikTok password.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the account, create and upload media, publish authorized posts, check publishing status, troubleshoot failures, and maintain the security of the integration. We do not sell personal information.

4. Third-party services

The App may use TikTok, Composio, and selected content-generation or hosting providers to perform these functions. Those services process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements. Only connect providers actually used in your deployment.

5. Children's privacy

Although the videos are designed to be educational and entertaining for children, the App is intended for operation by adults. It is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. We do not knowingly solicit names, contact details, or other personal information from children through the App. If you believe a child's personal information was submitted, contact us to request its removal.

6. Data retention and security

Account credentials and access tokens should be held by authorized integration providers using appropriate security controls. Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes, legal obligations, and troubleshooting, then deleted or de-identified where appropriate. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You can disconnect the App from your TikTok account through TikTok's connected-app settings or the relevant integration dashboard. You may contact the operator to request information about personal data held, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable law.

8. International processing

Connected service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Applicable safeguards and provider terms govern such processing.

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the App, connected services, or legal requirements. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
